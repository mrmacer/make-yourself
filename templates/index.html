Cloudflare Tunnel Setup

To access this app on your iPhone from home:

  1. Install cloudflared: brew install cloudflared
  2. Login: cloudflared tunnel login
  3. Create tunnel: cloudflared tunnel create reclaim
  4. Run: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:5050 run reclaim
  5. Open the generated URL on your iPhone and tap Share → Add to Home Screen

For home network only (no tunnel), just visit http://<your-mac-ip>:5050 on your phone while on Wi-Fi.
